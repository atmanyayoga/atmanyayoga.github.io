Membership summary

Key details to help you choose a plan with clarity.

Small-group classes

Separate batches for India (IST) and Europe (CET/CEST) for beginners and intermediate practitioners.

Terms

Membership terms and conditions

  1. Membership begins on the confirmed enrolment start date.
  2. Each subscription provides access to the selected batch.
  3. Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual memberships are valid for their respective subscription periods.
  4. Memberships are not automatically renewed. Students must make a fresh payment before their membership expires.
  5. Students are encouraged to attend all scheduled sessions.
  6. Missed classes due to personal reasons are not automatically refundable or transferable.
  7. If a class is cancelled by the teacher, a replacement session or appropriate extension will be arranged.
  8. Students should inform the teacher of any relevant injuries, medical limitations or physical concerns before participating.
  9. Students should practise within their own physical capacity and avoid forcing postures.
  10. Refund and cancellation requests will be handled according to the published refund policy and applicable consumer laws.
