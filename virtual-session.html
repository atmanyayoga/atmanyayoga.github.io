What to expect

Choose your 5-minute practice

  • Calm Breath: Slow breathing, gentle release, and stillness for balance.
  • Morning Ease: Awakening breath, light movement, and focus to start the day.
  • Evening Unwind: Soothing breath and gentle release to relax and settle.
  • Each mode includes voice guidance and calming background music (toggle anytime).
Before you begin
  • Sit comfortably on a mat or chair with your spine upright.
  • Keep your shoulders relaxed and your jaw soft.
  • Breathe gently and stay within a comfortable range.
  • If you feel discomfort, ease out of the movement and return to natural breathing.

This short practice is for general wellbeing and is not a substitute for medical advice. Please practise within your own capacity.
