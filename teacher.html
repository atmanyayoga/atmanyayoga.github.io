Meet Haasanthi Madala

Yoga Practitioner | Traditional Hatha Yoga Teacher

Journey & Training

A student of the path

With a background in engineering, my journey into yoga has been one of continuous learning, exploration and self-discovery.

What began as a physical practice gradually became a way of understanding the body, breath and mind, and looking inward.

I completed my 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training in Rishikesh in September 2024, under the traditional Hatha Yoga system.

My personal practice continues to be the foundation of everything I share. I believe yoga is not something we perform for an hour on the mat, but a discipline that gradually transforms the way we live, think and experience life.

Through Ātmanya Yoga, I hope to share traditional Hatha Yoga practices in their essence, making them accessible to sincere practitioners at different stages of their journey.

I am a student of this path, learning and practising every day.

Teaching Philosophy

Rooted in tradition, guided with care

An approach grounded in sincerity, awareness, and steady progress—honouring where each practitioner is.

Traditional & holistic

Classes integrate yama, niyama, āsana, prāṇāyāma, dhāraṇā, dhyāna and samādhi, reflecting the balanced essence of traditional Hatha Yoga.

Practice over perfection

Emphasis on awareness, breath, and consistency rather than achieving ideal shapes or comparison with others.

Safe & supportive

Small-group online sessions encourage mindful pacing, individual attention, and practice within one’s own capacity.

Beginner-friendly

Clear guidance for those starting out, while offering depth and steadiness for continuing practitioners.

Beyond the mat

Yoga as a discipline that gently influences how we breathe, think, and live—cultivating calm and clarity.

Lifelong learning

Teaching flows from ongoing personal practice and humility as a student of the path.
