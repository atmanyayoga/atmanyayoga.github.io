Classes

Online Traditional Hatha Yoga Group Classes

Live online sessions integrating āsana, prāṇāyāma, concentration and relaxation in a calm and structured learning environment.

01 · Classes

What to expect

These live online group sessions are designed for beginners and intermediate practitioners, with small groups that allow for guidance, consistency and individual attention.

  • 2 live sessions every week
  • 90 minutes per session
  • Small group setting
Good to know: Guidance is offered in a supportive, mindful manner. Practise within your own capacity and inform the teacher of any injuries or medical concerns.

Each session includes

  • Āsana — physical postures to develop strength, flexibility and stability
  • Prāṇāyāma — breathing practices to regulate and deepen awareness of the breath
  • Dhāraṇā — concentration practices to cultivate focus and clarity
  • Śavāsana — guided relaxation to integrate the effects of the practice
02 · A Typical 90-Minute Session

Class flow

A thoughtful structure that supports the body, breath, and mind from arrival to integration.

  • Arrival & centering

    Settling in, posture, and gentle breath awareness to arrive in the present moment.

  • Warm-up & āsana

    Mindful movement and postures to build strength, stability, flexibility, and body awareness.

  • Prāṇāyāma

    Guided breathing practices to regulate and deepen awareness of the breath.

  • Dhāraṇā

    Concentration practices to cultivate focus and clarity of mind.

  • Śavāsana

    Guided relaxation to integrate the effects of the practice in stillness.

  • Closing

    Brief integration and closing, carrying awareness beyond the mat.

Which batch suits me?

  • Beginner: Ideal if you are new to yoga or returning after a break and want clear, steady guidance.
  • Intermediate: Suitable if you have an existing practice and wish to deepen with consistent traditional Hatha Yoga.
  • Region/time zone: Choose India (IST) or Europe (CET/CEST) based on your location and availability.

What you’ll need for online classes

  • A yoga mat and a quiet, well-lit space to practise safely
  • Stable internet connection and a device for Google Meet
  • Comfortable clothing and water nearby
  • Willingness to practise within your capacity and inform the teacher of any concerns

FAQ

Common questions about classes

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Beginner batches are designed for those new to yoga or returning after a break, with clear guidance in a small-group setting.

What if I miss a class?

Students are encouraged to attend all scheduled sessions. Missed classes due to personal reasons are not automatically refundable or transferable. If a class is cancelled by the teacher, a replacement session or appropriate extension will be arranged.

Can I practise with an injury or limitation?

Please inform the teacher of any relevant injuries, medical limitations or physical concerns before participating. Practise within your own capacity and avoid forcing postures. Seek medical advice where appropriate.

When will batch timings be confirmed?

Batch timings are currently listed as “To be confirmed”. Please submit an enquiry to receive the latest schedule and availability for your preferred region and level.
