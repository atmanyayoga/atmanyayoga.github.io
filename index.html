Our Approach

Yoga is more than physical postures.

At Ātmanya Yoga, we approach yoga as a discipline that extends beyond the physical body.

Rooted in traditional Hatha Yoga, our practices bring together āsana, prāṇāyāma, concentration and relaxation to help develop a deeper awareness of the body, breath and mind.

We believe that yoga is not about achieving the perfect posture or competing with others. It is a gradual process of learning, observing and turning inward through consistent practice.

“
Yoga is not about achieving the perfect posture. It is a gradual process of learning, observing and turning inward through consistent practice.
— The Ātmanya Yoga philosophy
Foundations

What is Traditional Hatha Yoga?

A balanced path that integrates body, breath, and mind—cultivating steadiness, clarity, and inner stillness through consistent practice.

आ

Āsana

Physical postures to develop strength, stability, flexibility, and mindful awareness of the body.

प्रा

Prāṇāyāma

Breathing practices that regulate and deepen awareness of the breath, supporting calm and vitality.

धा

Dhāraṇā

Concentration practices that nurture focus, clarity, and a steady, attentive mind.

ॐ

Dhyāna & Samādhi

Meditation and deep absorption that settle the mind into stillness and integration.

✦
Benefits

What a steady practice can support

Grounded, gradual benefits that develop over time with regular and mindful practice. Progress is personal and individual.

Strength & stability

Build balanced physical strength, posture support, and a steadier foundation in movement.

Flexibility & ease

Encourage mobility and ease in the body through mindful, unforced practice.

Breath awareness

Develop a calmer, more conscious relationship with the breath in daily life.

Focus & clarity

Support concentration and mental clarity through dhāraṇā and attentive practice.

Calm & balance

Nurture relaxation and a sense of inner balance through consistent, holistic sessions.

Routine & discipline

Foster a sustainable practice rhythm that supports overall wellbeing over time.

The Teacher

Meet Haasanthi Madala

Ātmanya Yoga is guided by Haasanthi Madala — Yoga Practitioner and Traditional Hatha Yoga Teacher.

With a background in engineering, her journey into yoga has been one of continuous learning, exploration and self-discovery. She completed her 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training in Rishikesh in September 2024, under the traditional Hatha Yoga system.

Her teaching is rooted in sincere personal practice, making traditional Hatha Yoga accessible to practitioners at different stages of their journey.
